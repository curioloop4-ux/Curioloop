# FANCAMVERSE Brand Guide

## Brand position
A high-energy, English-language K-pop fancam Shorts destination for US and international fans.

## Core line
**EVERY IDOL. EVERY MOMENT.**

## Voice
- Energetic, concise, fan-first.
- English titles and descriptions.
- Identify group, member, song, and event only from uploader-provided metadata.
- Avoid clickbait claims that are not visible in the footage.

## Visual system
- Midnight navy: #090B25
- Electric violet: #713BFF
- Neon magenta: #FF2FAD
- Spotlight white: #F7F7FF
- Accent red recording dot: #FF3158

## Logo direction
FV monogram, camera/viewfinder brackets, orbiting light trail, concert spotlights. Keep the icon legible at small avatar sizes.

## Channel description
Your favorite K-pop idols, unforgettable stage moments, and the best fancam highlights. Discover incredible performances, iconic expressions, and legendary moments from K-pop stages. Subscribe and experience every moment!

## Channel keywords
K-pop, Kpop, fancam, K-pop fancam, idol fancam, K-pop performance, K-pop Shorts, stage performance, dance performance, idol moments

## Publishing style
- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Initial target length: 18–35 seconds; current processor default is 24 seconds.
- Keep original performance audio unless the uploader explicitly requests otherwise.
- No narration by default.
- Avoid large permanent captions that obscure the performer.
- Review every output for framing, audio clipping, accidental black frames, and rights before publishing.
